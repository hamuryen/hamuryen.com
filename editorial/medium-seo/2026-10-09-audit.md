## Content issues to address before further promotion

- The English and Turkish ECG/EKG posts describe microphones converting heart sounds into electrical signals. ECG electrodes record electrical activity. These two posts need a factual content revision and review of the project explanation; their SEO fields were left unchanged. Reference: [NHS ECG explanation](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/electrocardiogram/).
- The Copilot story's visible title and introductory deadline are tied to April 2026. Its SEO description now explicitly identifies it as an April 2026 guide. A future body update should distinguish the effective policy date from the ongoing opt-out. Verified original announcement: [GitHub interaction data policy](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/updates-to-github-copilot-interaction-data-usage-policy/).
- The microservices essay leads with a “2025 CNCF survey of 689 organizations” and 42% statistic. A targeted primary-source search did not establish this statistic; verify the original citation before using it for promotion. New metadata does not repeat the statistic. This is not a full factual audit of all 24 articles.
- “Hello world!” is a two-sentence introductory post without an informative search target; unchanged.
